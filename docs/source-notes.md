# Initial source notes

Source: https://mfkbuilder.com/ — reviewed September 12, 2026.

- Farhan Residency's dedicated page confirms PIA Housing Society, Scheme 33. It describes 3-room apartments and the amenities used in the site. Current availability and dates are not established.
- Muhammad Farhan Khan is identified as CEO.
- The company describes work in real estate consultancy, residential development, commercial construction, and housing societies.
- Its history names Madras Society, ROK Society, State Bank Society, Bab-e-Rehmat, and PIA Society under delivered projects. This wording does not establish that MFK developed each entire society; verify the specific developments before creating project pages.
